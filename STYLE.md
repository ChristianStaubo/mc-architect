# Style rules

How we build. The builder reads this before every build; the critic's rubric (`evals/RUBRIC.md`)
scores against the same ideas. Rules come from failures and from the user ("rule: ..."): each new rule
gets a date and, where it can be checked, a check in `mc audit` or a test.

## Shape
- No plain boxes. Break the footprint (an L, a wing, a porch) or the roofline (dormers, a cross gable,
  a tower) on anything bigger than 7 x 7.
- Roofs overhang the walls by at least one block and have a visible pitch; finish the ridge and the
  gable ends.

## Depth
- No flat walls longer than 4 blocks: add a frame (logs at corners and every 3–5 blocks), a beam band
  under the eaves or between floors, inset windows (set the glass one block in), or trim.
- Foundations are a deliberate band (stone, 1–2 blocks visible), not a cliff. Where the ground drops
  more than 3 blocks, use posts, stilts or a terrace instead of a solid wall of cobblestone.

## Palette
- 3–5 materials per build: a structure (logs/stone), a fill (planks/plaster/brick), a roof, an accent.
  Vary big surfaces with 2–3 similar blocks (stone bricks + cracked + mossy).

## People-sized
- Doors 2 high, ceilings 3+ blocks in main rooms (2 is cramped), windows 1–2 wide and 2 tall on
  ground floors. Stairs a player can walk up (no gaps, 2 blocks of headroom over every step).
- Every indoor floor is lit (the audit fails dark spots): lanterns, torches or hidden glowstone.

## Site
- Use the slope: step floors, add a basement or terrace, don't flatten everything.
- A path to every door. No floating blocks, no leftover half-trees.
