# Town rubric (the town critic)

| Criterion | High | Low |
|---|---|---|
| layout | Streets go somewhere: a centre where routes meet, a hierarchy (high street, lanes), plots front the road. | A grid, or buildings scattered with no street logic. |
| land | Follows the terrain: terraces, roads on contours, water used (quays, bridge, boathouses). | Flattened pads, roads ignoring the slope, scars and cliffs of foundation. |
| unity | One place: a shared palette and roof language, with variation in size and detail. | Every building a different style, or all clones. |
| landmarks | A few buildings stand out and are placed where they're seen (church on the crest, lighthouse on the point). | Nothing to orient by. |
| density | Feels lived in: gaps between buildings are gardens, yards, walls, trees, not bare grass. | Islands of buildings in empty lawn. |
| connections | Every door reaches a road; roads join; the bridge lands somewhere sensible. | Dead ends, doors to nowhere. |
| skyline | Varied heights and roofs read well from the water and from across the lake. | Flat, uniform. |

Output JSON: {"town", "scores": {criterion: 1-10}, "overall": 1-10, "summary", "problems": [3 concrete
fixes, most important first], "strengths": [...]}.
